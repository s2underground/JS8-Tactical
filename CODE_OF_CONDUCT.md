# Code of conduct

JS8-Tactical adopts the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**
as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull
requests, discussions, the project's email, and anywhere someone represents the project.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome newcomers and operators of
every experience level, and keep criticism about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults,
personal attacks and publishing someone's private information (including their location or
callsign-to-identity details they haven't made public) are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to **js8tactical@proton.me** with "CONDUCT" in the subject.
Reports are read by the maintainer only and kept confidential. If a report concerns the
maintainer, say so in the subject; it will be handled as openly as the report allows, and you may
also contact GitHub through its reporting tools.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (see [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)) enforces this code using the Contributor
Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, or permanent ban, according
to the impact of the behaviour. Decisions are explained to the person concerned.
