As the radio operator for a group, your job is to know who is out there, who needs what, and who you can reach. JS8-Tactical is built for that.

# 1. Set up your station first
Everything here assumes you can already send and receive. If not, start with the station guide.

[[guide:start-operator|Setting up your station]]

# 2. Put your team on the Team tab
Add each member's callsign, a name or role, and a section (for example "Alpha" or "Medical"). Add a grid square for anyone you haven't heard yet; it's replaced by their real grid once you hear them.

The Team tab shows, for each person:
- when you last heard them and how strong they were,
- their latest **CHECKIN** or **SITREP** (red for an emergency, amber for a need),
- a stoplight for each band showing how likely you are to reach them **right now**, from the propagation model.

[[app:team|Open the Team tab]]

# 3. Use a group
A **group** is a name starting with @ (for example @TEAM). Messages to a group reach everyone in it, and their traffic stays together on the Traffic tab. Agree on one group name with your team and enter it under Settings, My Groups.

[[app:settings|Settings: My Groups]]

# 4. Ask for reports, not chat
Short structured reports save airtime and are easy to act on:
- **#CHECKIN** status (GOOD / NEED / EMERG), location and a note.
- **#SITREP** status (GREEN / AMBER / RED), location, needs and notes.
- **#SPOT** and **#SALUTE** for something seen: size, activity, location, time.
- **#POSREP** for where someone is going.

Received reports show as cards on Traffic, as points on the Map (and in ATAK if you turn that on), and feed the Team tab and the Digest. Stations using ordinary JS8Call can still read them as plain text.

# 5. Check the Digest
The Digest gathers the last hours or days into one page: open needs and emergencies first, then messages to you that nobody answered, reports, and which stations were heard. Share it as text to hand over to the next shift.

[[app:digest|Open the Digest]]

# 6. Plan for the band changing
Propagation changes through the day. Before a scheduled check-in, look at the Team tab's stoplights and the Prop tab's band chart, and agree a primary and a backup band with your team (for example 40 m by day, 80 m at night).

[[app:prop|Prop tab: best band by distance and time]]

> TIP: Message history is kept on this tablet (7 days by default). Change how long, or clear it, under Settings, Message history.
